The Concept

Where Everyday Life Slows Down

CAFE STORE was conceived around a simple yet profound premise: that a modern coffee shop should be an oasis from the bustling city. A sanctuary where you can catch your breath, celebrate a slow morning with a rich pour-over, or engage in meaningful conversations with friends.

We avoid industrial shortcuts. Every coffee bean is roasted in small batches, our bakery dough undergoes natural fermentation, and our sandwiches are assembled with farm-fresh produce and genuine dairy cheeses.

Sourcing & Roasting

Our Coffee Preparation Approach

We select high-altitude Arabica crops cultivated under native shade canopies. The beans are processed with meticulous attention to moisture levels before arriving at our roastery.

Our profile development targets balanced caramel sweetness, low bitter astringency, and subtle undertones of roasted hazelnut and dark chocolate. Every morning, baristas dial in the espresso grinders according to current humidity and ambient temperature to guarantee standard extraction perfection.

Precision Ratio

1:2 espresso brew ratio extracted within 26–30 seconds.

Velvet Milk

Steamed gently to 62°C–65°C to preserve natural sweetness.

Hospitality

The Barista Experience

A great cup of coffee requires human devotion. Our baristas are passionate beverage artisans who understand the chemistry of extraction, the geometry of pouring latte art, and the art of warm hospitality.

Whether you prefer your Americano extra strong, your latte with almond milk, or want a recommendation for a sweet pairing with our freshly baked croissants, our team is eager to craft your ideal order.
